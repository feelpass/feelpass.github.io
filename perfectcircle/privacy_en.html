Privacy Policy

Perfect Circle - Draw & Compete

1. Information We Collect

Perfect Circle collects the following information to provide game functionality and online features:

  • Google account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, and profile photo to create your in-game profile. This is used solely for identification on the leaderboard.
  • Game scores and records: Your circle drawing scores are stored locally on your device and, if you sign in, synced to our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) for ranking purposes.
  • Team and profile data: Your chosen team name and country are stored in Firebase Firestore for leaderboard display.
  • Analytics data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, feature usage, and crash reports. This data does not include personally identifiable information.
  • Advertising ID: Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your preferences.

2. Permissions We Request

Perfect Circle requests minimal permissions:

  • Internet: Required for Google sign-in, syncing scores to the leaderboard, serving ads, and analytics.
  • Vibrate: Used for haptic feedback while drawing circles, providing a tactile response based on drawing accuracy.

3. Third-Party Services

Perfect Circle uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Authentication: Handles Google sign-in. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Firebase Firestore: Stores leaderboard data (display name, team, country, scores). See Firebase Privacy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous usage statistics. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Play Services: Provides sign-in functionality. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide the circle drawing game and score calculation
  • To display global and monthly leaderboards
  • To identify players on the ranking board
  • To display advertisements
  • To analyze app usage and improve user experience

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local game data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Leaderboard data stored in Firebase Firestore can be deleted upon request by contacting us.
  • Analytics data is retained by Firebase according to Google's data retention policies.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • You can sign out of your Google account within the app at any time.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data:

  • Local game records are stored in the app's private database
  • Communication with Firebase services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Google sign-in is handled through secure OAuth 2.0 protocols
  • No passwords are stored by the app

7. Children's Privacy

Perfect Circle does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

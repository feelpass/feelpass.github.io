Privacy Policy

Gemini Eraser - AI Watermark Remover

Key Point: Gemini Eraser processes all images entirely on your device. Your images are never uploaded to any external server.

1. Information We Collect

Gemini Eraser is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect minimal information:

  • Image data: Images you select for watermark removal are processed entirely on your device. They are never transmitted to any server.
  • App preferences: Settings such as theme preference and processing history count are stored only on your device's local storage.
  • Purchase information: If you purchase the premium plan, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment details.
  • Usage & diagnostics data: We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous app usage events and crash diagnostics. This data helps us improve the app and is not used to personally identify you.

2. Permissions We Request

Gemini Eraser requests only the minimum permissions necessary:

  • Photo/Media access: Required to read images for watermark removal and to save processed images to your device.
  • Internet: Used solely for serving advertisements and processing in-app purchases. Image processing does not require internet access.

3. Offline Processing

All watermark detection and removal processing is performed 100% on your device using local algorithms. Specifically:

  • No images are uploaded to any cloud service or remote server.
  • No image data is transmitted over the network.
  • The watermark removal engine operates entirely offline.
  • The app functions fully without an internet connection (ads and purchases excluded).

4. Third-Party Services

Gemini Eraser uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements to free-tier users. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes premium plan purchases. Purchase information is handled by Google Play. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous app usage events (such as feature interactions) to help us understand how the app is used. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports and diagnostic information to improve app stability. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide watermark detection and removal functionality
  • To save your app preferences locally
  • To display advertisements (free-tier users)
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To analyze app usage and improve stability (analytics and crash diagnostics)

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All local data (preferences, processing history) is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Processed images are saved only where you choose to save them on your device.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your data:

  • All image processing occurs locally — no data leaves your device.
  • App preferences are stored in the app's private storage area, inaccessible to other apps.
  • Communication with third-party services (ads, billing) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Children's Privacy

Gemini Eraser does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since all image processing is performed locally and no personal data is collected, there is minimal risk of children's data being exposed.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

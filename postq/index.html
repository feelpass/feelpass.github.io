postq

A personal social media scheduling tool by plantcode

postq is a small tool that the developer of plantcode uses to schedule and publish posts to the developer's own social media accounts. It runs on the developer's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Publishes scheduled posts (Reels, images, carousels, Shorts, text posts) to the developer's own Instagram, Threads and YouTube accounts through each platform's official API.
  • Collects basic statistics (views, likes, comments) for those posts.
  • Lists comments on those posts so the developer can reply to them.

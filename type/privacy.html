Privacy Policy

TypeAction

1. Information We Collect

TypeAction does not collect any personal information. All data is stored locally on your device.

  • Shortcut data: Stored only in the device's internal storage
  • App settings: Stored only in the device's internal storage
  • No personal data is transmitted to any server

2. Accessibility Service

TypeAction uses the Android Accessibility Service API to provide its core functionality:

  • Detecting text input to trigger shortcut replacements
  • Performing text replacement in the active text field
  • Displaying suggestion popups for matching shortcuts

The Accessibility Service is used solely for the app's text replacement functionality. No data from other apps is collected, stored, or transmitted.

3. Use of Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no applicable use of personal data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also export and import your shortcuts as files for backup purposes.

5. Third-Party Sharing

TypeAction does not share any user data with third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

TypeAction may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device information for ad personalization. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases. Purchase data is handled by Google Play.
  • Firebase: For app analytics and crash reporting. No personally identifiable information is collected.

7. Children's Privacy

TypeAction does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

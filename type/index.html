Stored on your device,
processed on your device

  • Your shortcuts, your settings and the text you type are stored and processed on your device. The developer has no server that receives them.
  • The text you type is never sent to any server.
  • The accessibility service is what runs your shortcuts: it detects what you type, swaps in the text or runs the action, and shows suggestions.
  • Type sends a copy of your shortcuts off your device only if you export them to a file or turn on Google Drive backup yourself.

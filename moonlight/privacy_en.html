Privacy Policy

Moonlight - Soft Flashlight & Moon

Summary: Moonlight is a free utility app supported by ads. It uses Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Google AdMob (banner & interstitial), and Google Play Billing (in-app purchases). Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play — Moonlight never sees them. Your approximate location is used only on-device for moon calculations and never sent to any server.

1. Information We Collect

Moonlight collects the following information through integrated third-party SDKs to deliver a free, supported experience and to improve quality. The app itself does not run any custom servers.

1.1 Automatically collected (Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics)

  • App usage events: screen views, feature usage, session length
  • Device info: model, OS version, language, country, screen resolution
  • Crash logs: stack traces and device state on abnormal termination
  • Firebase Installation ID (FID) and a non-identifying app instance ID

This information is processed in anonymous/aggregated form and is not used to identify you personally. See Firebase Privacy Information.

1.2 Advertising identifier (Google AdMob)

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) — for frequency capping and personalized/non-personalized ad delivery
  • IP address (used for coarse geolocation by Google's ad systems)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

Moonlight contains banner and interstitial ads. See Google Ads Policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads from Settings → Google → Ads.

1.3 Payment information (Google Play Billing)

Moonlight may offer in-app purchases (e.g. ad removal, pro features).

  • All payments are processed directly by Google Play Store. Moonlight never receives or stores payment method information such as credit card numbers.
  • Moonlight only receives the purchase token and SKU (product ID) to confirm purchase status.
  • Receipt verification uses Google Play's standard libraries.

1.4 Location (optional)

  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) is used to compute accurate moon phase, moonrise and moonset times for your area.
  • Your location is processed entirely on-device and is never sent to any server, including not to Firebase Analytics. (Analytics may automatically log a coarse country/city derived by Google from your IP, which is separate.)
  • You may instead pick a city manually without granting this permission.

4. How We Use Information

  • To deliver core features (flashlight, screen light, timer, moon information)
  • To serve ads that fund the free tier
  • To process in-app purchases such as ad removal and to restore purchases
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability
  • To analyze anonymous feature-usage statistics

Information is never sold, used for marketing emails, or used to build personal profiles beyond what Google's services do as part of standard ad delivery.

6. Children's Privacy

Moonlight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If a child uses the app, we recommend configuring AdMob to serve only non-personalized ads (planned for a future build).

7. Effect of Denying Permissions

  • Camera denied: The LED torch (flashlight) cannot be used. The screen moonlight mode still works.
  • Location denied: Automatic moon info is unavailable. Pick a city manually instead.
  • Notifications denied: The foreground service notification is hidden, but core features still work.
  • Exact alarm denied: Sleep timer accuracy may be slightly reduced.
  • No network: Ads will not show and purchases cannot be restored. All offline features still work.

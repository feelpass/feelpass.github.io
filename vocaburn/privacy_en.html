Privacy Policy

Vocaburn · com.coco.vocaburn

한국어 버전

Vocaburn (the "App") respects your privacy and handles data as described below.

The conditions for using the service are set out in the separate Terms of Use.

Summary: Signing in is optional. Without an account, your word list and study history stay only on your device. If you sign in with Google, your word list is stored on the developer's server (Seoul, South Korea region) so it syncs across your devices, and you can delete your account and the server data linked to it at any time from Settings (see 5 for what remains in study groups). The words, meanings and notes you enter are never sent to advertising or analytics services. However, if you use word auto-fill (on by default, can be turned off in Settings), the term you type is sent to the developer's server to look up its meaning whether or not you are signed in, and if the server has no result, it may be passed to Anthropic to generate meanings and examples (1-5). If you join a study group, the messages and word sets you add are shown to members of the same group with your nickname (your test results are shown with your nickname to members of the same group only in groups where the owner keeps test results public, and to the owner regardless of that setting), and notifications about new messages and word sets are sent to your device (1-6, 9).

1. Information we collect

1-1. Account information (when you sign in with Google)

  • Basic Google profile — email address, name and profile picture URL. The App requests only the email and profile scopes and does not access any other Google account data (contacts, Drive, etc.).
  • Nickname and emoji — your Google account name is used as the default nickname and can be changed freely in the App. Your nickname is shown as the author on word sets you publish (both public and unlisted). In study groups your nickname and emoji are shown to members of the same group, and for groups you own your nickname is also shown in group search (only for groups you keep visible in search) and invite-code previews, which any signed-in user can use (1-6).
  • Lifetime status and purchase records — whether Lifetime (one-time in-app purchase) is active is stored so the same benefits apply on all your devices. When you buy or restore Lifetime, the server checks with Google Play that the payment is valid and stores the product ID, purchase token, order ID, purchase state, purchase and verification times, and the purchase details returned by Google Play (such as the billing region code). If Google Play sends a refund or cancellation notification, the purchase state is updated.
  • Terms of Use and privacy policy consent record — when you agree while signing in, or when you open the App after the Terms of Use or this policy has changed, the document you agreed to (the Terms of Use or this privacy policy) and its version, the time you agreed and the device ID of that device are stored.

1-3. Shared word sets (when you publish on the web version)

  • When you publish a word set on the web version (vocaburn.vercel.app), its title, description, language and a snapshot of the words at that time (words, meanings, notes, examples) are stored on the server.
  • A public set can be searched and viewed by other users and shows your nickname as the author. An unlisted set can only be opened by people who know its share code.
  • When you import someone else's set, a subscription record (which set, when) is stored. Only you can see your subscriptions.
  • You can delete (unpublish) sets you published on the web version. A deleted set disappears from the lists, and the records left on the server (its title, description, copy of the words and so on) are deleted when you delete your account.

1-4. Automatically collected information

  • Usage statistics — anonymized events such as screen views and feature usage (Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Crash information — device model, OS version and error logs when the App crashes (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Advertising identifier — the advertising ID and ad interaction data used to show ads to free users (Google AdMob)

None of this includes the words, meanings or notes you enter.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Location — the App neither requests nor uses location permissions.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone — the App does not request these permissions. Pronunciation playback (TTS) uses your device's speech engine and does not record audio.
  • Payment details — card numbers and other payment information are handled by Google Play; the App never sees them.

3. Data stored only on your device

  • The entire word list, groups, collections, study history and stats of users who are not signed in (for terms sent by word auto-fill, see 1-5)
  • Per-attempt study history of free users
  • App settings — theme, language, study options, reminder time, free trial start time

If you are not signed in, this data cannot be recovered when you delete the App or switch devices (it may be included in your Google account backup if Android Auto Backup is on). Lifetime users can use CSV export in Settings to back it up to a location they choose; the App does not access exported files.

4. How we use the data

  • Syncing your word list and stats across devices and restoring them on a new device
  • Providing the word set sharing feature
  • Providing study groups (joining groups, sharing word sets, group test results boards and chat) and sending group notifications
  • Reviewing study group reports and preventing abuse (rate limits)
  • Verifying Lifetime purchases and applying Lifetime benefits
  • Providing word auto-fill and AI study notes, and applying the daily usage limit
  • Confirming your consent to the Terms of Use and this privacy policy
  • Fixing errors and improving the App
  • Showing ads to free users

5. Retention and deletion

  • Synced data — kept until you delete your account. Words, groups and collections you delete in the App are kept in a deleted state so the deletion can propagate to your other devices, and are periodically removed permanently from the server after 30 days.
  • Account deletion — tap Settings > Account > Delete account in the App. Your account, profile, synced data (including device info), Terms of Use and privacy policy consent records, purchase records, published word sets and, in study groups, your memberships, the messages you sent, notices showing your nickname, your test results, reports you made, block records (both those you made and those against you) and removal records about you are deleted from the server immediately and cannot be recovered (your account ID in read and removal signals left in the real-time delivery log is deleted within about 4 days). The words, groups, collections, study history and stats stored on the device you deleted the account from are deleted as well. Data stored on other devices signed in to the same account, and data on your devices when the account is deleted by email request, is not deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records — kept until you delete your account. However, refund records sent by Google Play (the notification as received, including purchase token, order ID and refund type) are used to make sure a refunded purchase is not treated as valid again; they are kept without an account ID and are not deleted when you delete your account.
  • Signing out — signing out alone does not delete server data. However, signing out deletes the words, groups, collections, study history and stats stored on that device, and the data synced to the server is restored when you sign in again. Data not synced to the server by the time you sign out cannot be restored; if unsynced words remain, the App shows how many and asks you to confirm before signing out.
  • Study groups — study group messages, word sets and test results are kept until the group is dissolved and permanently deleted, but are deleted earlier in the following cases. When the sender or the owner deletes a message, it is removed from the server's message records right away. When the uploader or the owner deletes a word set, it is no longer shown to members, and after 30 days it is permanently deleted from the server, together with its test results, by a weekly cleanup (within about 37 days). Chat notices about that word set being added or updated and about its test results (including the title, word count, scores and nicknames) are not deleted then and stay in the group. When you delete your account, your messages and test results are deleted as described under "Account deletion" above. When a group is dissolved, it is no longer shown to members, and after 30 days its memberships, messages, notices, word sets, test results, removal records and reports are all permanently deleted from the server by a weekly cleanup (within about 37 days). You cannot delete your messages in the App in groups you have left or been removed from (if such a group is still active, they stay until it is dissolved and permanently deleted) or in dissolved groups, or the messages you sent to a group before rejoining it; these messages are deleted from all groups when you delete your account (in the App or by email request). Even after you delete your account, word sets you uploaded to a group stay in the group with only the uploader removed, removal records you made stay with only the remover removed, and reports other users made about you are kept for review. Words that other members saved to their own word lists from a word set or from missed test words ("Save to my wordbook") stay in those members' word lists (and in their synced data if they sync) even if the word set or your account is deleted. For groups you own, ownership passes automatically to the editor who joined earliest, or, if there is no editor, to the member who joined earliest; if there are no other members, the group is dissolved. If you leave a group without deleting your account, the messages you sent, word sets you uploaded and your test results stay in the group. Notification text in the sending queue is deleted by a weekly cleanup once 7 days have passed since it was sent or discarded after 5 failed attempts; notification text that has not been sent is deleted when the original message (for word set notifications, the notice announcing the added set) is deleted or the group is permanently deleted. Rate-limit counters are periodically deleted after 2 days.
  • Usage and crash data — retained according to Firebase's default retention policies.
  • Word auto-fill — the per-term cache of generated results is shared data not linked to any user, so it is not deleted when you delete your account. Daily usage limit records (device ID, date, lookup count) are periodically deleted after 7 days.

If you can no longer access the App, you can request account deletion by email. See Delete Your Account and Data for details.

6. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, each of which may process limited data on its own servers:

  • Supabase — hosts account authentication, the synced, shared and study group data, real-time delivery of study group chat, the word auto-fill server (Edge Function), pronunciation audio files and the study group notification sending server. The server is located in the Seoul, South Korea region (ap-northeast-2); synced data can be accessed only by the signed-in owner, and the server returns study group data only to members of that group (for what group search and invite-code previews show, see 1-6; for real-time chat, membership is checked when the connection is opened, and if you are removed or the group is dissolved while the chat screen is open, the App closes that connection). (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — account authentication. Only your email, name and profile picture are provided.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads to free users. To serve ads it may collect the advertising identifier and ad interaction data and share them with Google's ad network. In regions that require consent (e.g. the EEA) a consent screen is shown on first launch and can be changed later in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the one-time Lifetime purchase. The developer's server verifies payments with the Google Play Developer API and receives refund and cancellation notifications from Google Play. Google Play keeps the original purchase history; the server stores the purchase records described in 1-1.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymized usage statistics, used only to improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash information, used only to improve stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads configuration values such as update notices. No personal data is sent.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — issues a device token for receiving push notifications and delivers study group notifications to your device. The App stores this token on the server only when you are signed in; when a notification is sent, the device token, the notification text (the group name and the sender's nickname with the beginning of the message or the word set title) and group and message identifiers are passed to Firebase.
  • Anthropic — generates word auto-fill results and AI study notes. Only the term you type may be passed to the Anthropic API, and only when the server has no stored result; your device ID and account information are not passed on.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google services process data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. International transfers

The Supabase server that stores synced and shared data is located in South Korea (Seoul). Google Sign-In, AdMob and Firebase (including delivery of study group notifications) run on Google's global infrastructure, so your data may be processed on servers outside your country. Terms passed to Anthropic by word auto-fill may be processed on servers in the United States.

8. Security

All communication between the App and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Server data is protected by row-level access control so that only the signed-in owner can access their synced data, and shared word sets are exposed only according to the visibility you chose. The server returns study group data only after checking that the requesting user is a member of that group (the information shown in group search and invite-code previews can be seen by any signed-in user, as described in 1-6, and for real-time chat, membership is checked when the connection is opened), and invite passwords are stored only as irreversible hashes.

9. Notifications

Study reminders, word alerts and the autoplay media notification are local notifications scheduled and shown on your device. The only push notifications sent from the server are study group notifications about new messages and new word sets, delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging. You are not notified about messages you send or word sets you upload, groups you have muted, or messages and word sets from users you have blocked. You can mute group notifications for each group on the group screen, and turn off all notifications at any time in your device settings.

10. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete your data in the App (editing your word list, CSV export (Lifetime), account deletion), stop syncing by signing out, delete your messages, leave groups, mute group notifications, and block and report users in study groups, and stop auto-fill and AI study notes lookup requests by turning off word auto-fill in Settings (pronunciation audio for words that already have AI study notes is still downloaded from the server when played, regardless of this setting). For any other access, correction or deletion request, contact us at the email below and we will respond without undue delay.

11. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

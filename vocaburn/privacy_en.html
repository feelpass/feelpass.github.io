Privacy Policy

Vocaburn · com.coco.vocaburn

한국어 버전

Vocaburn (the "App") respects your privacy and handles data as described below.

Summary: Signing in is optional. Without an account, your word list and study history stay only on your device. If you sign in with Google, your word list is stored on the developer's server (Seoul, South Korea region) so it syncs across your devices, and you can delete your account and all server data linked to it at any time from Settings. The words, meanings and notes you enter are never sent to advertising or analytics services. However, if you use word auto-fill (on by default, can be turned off in Settings), the term you type is sent to the developer's server to look up its meaning whether or not you are signed in, and if the server has no result, it may be passed to Anthropic to generate meanings and examples (1-5).

1. Information we collect

1-1. Account information (when you sign in with Google)

  • Basic Google profile — email address, name and profile picture URL. The App requests only the email and profile scopes and does not access any other Google account data (contacts, Drive, etc.).
  • Nickname and emoji — your Google account name is used as the default nickname and can be changed freely in the App. Your nickname is shown as the author on word sets you publish as "public".
  • Lifetime status and purchase records — whether Lifetime (one-time in-app purchase) is active is stored so the same benefits apply on all your devices. When you buy or restore Lifetime, the server checks with Google Play that the payment is valid and stores the product ID, purchase token, order ID, purchase state, purchase and verification times, and the purchase details returned by Google Play (such as the billing region code). If Google Play sends a refund or cancellation notification, the purchase state is updated.
  • Privacy policy consent record — when you agree to this policy while signing in, or when you open the App after the policy has changed, the policy version you agreed to, the time you agreed and the device ID of that device are stored.

When signed in, the following data is stored on the server and synced with other devices signed in to the same account.

1-3. Shared word sets (when you publish on the web version)

  • When you publish a word set on the web version (vocaburn.vercel.app), its title, description, language and a snapshot of the words at that time (words, meanings, notes, examples) are stored on the server.
  • A public set can be searched and viewed by other users and shows your nickname as the author. An unlisted set can only be opened by people who know its share code.
  • When you import someone else's set, a subscription record (which set, when) is stored. Only you can see your subscriptions.
  • You can delete sets you published on the web version.

1-4. Automatically collected information

  • Usage statistics — anonymized events such as screen views and feature usage (Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Crash information — device model, OS version and error logs when the App crashes (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Advertising identifier — the advertising ID and ad interaction data used to show ads to free users (Google AdMob)

None of this includes the words, meanings or notes you enter.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Location — the App neither requests nor uses location permissions.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone — the App does not request these permissions. Pronunciation playback (TTS) uses your device's speech engine and does not record audio.
  • Payment details — card numbers and other payment information are handled by Google Play; the App never sees them.

3. Data stored only on your device

  • The entire word list, groups, collections, study history and stats of users who are not signed in (for terms sent by word auto-fill, see 1-5)
  • Per-attempt study history of free users
  • App settings — theme, language, study options, reminder time, free trial start time

If you are not signed in, this data cannot be recovered when you delete the App or switch devices (it may be included in your Google account backup if Android Auto Backup is on). Lifetime users can use CSV export in Settings to back it up to a location they choose; the App does not access exported files.

4. How we use the data

  • Syncing your word list and stats across devices and restoring them on a new device
  • Providing the word set sharing feature
  • Verifying Lifetime purchases and applying Lifetime benefits
  • Providing word auto-fill and AI study notes, and applying the daily usage limit
  • Confirming your consent to this privacy policy
  • Fixing errors and improving the App
  • Showing ads to free users

5. Retention and deletion

  • Synced data — kept until you delete your account. Words, groups and collections you delete in the App are kept in a deleted state so the deletion can propagate to your other devices, and are periodically removed permanently from the server after 30 days.
  • Account deletion — tap Settings > Account > Delete account in the App. Your account, profile, synced data (including device info), privacy policy consent records, purchase records and published word sets are deleted from the server immediately and cannot be recovered. The words, groups, collections, study history and stats stored on the device you deleted the account from are deleted as well. Data stored on other devices signed in to the same account, and data on your devices when the account is deleted by email request, is not deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records — kept until you delete your account. However, refund records sent by Google Play (the notification as received, including purchase token, order ID and refund type) are used to make sure a refunded purchase is not treated as valid again; they are kept without an account ID and are not deleted when you delete your account.
  • Signing out — signing out alone does not delete server data; it is restored when you sign in again.
  • Usage and crash data — retained according to Firebase's default retention policies.
  • Word auto-fill — the per-term cache of generated results is shared data not linked to any user, so it is not deleted when you delete your account. Daily usage limit records (device ID, date, lookup count) are periodically deleted after 7 days.

If you can no longer access the App, you can request account deletion by email. See Delete Your Account and Data for details.

6. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, each of which may process limited data on its own servers:

  • Supabase — hosts account authentication, the synced/shared data, the word auto-fill server (Edge Function) and pronunciation audio files. The server is located in the Seoul, South Korea region (ap-northeast-2), and database access is restricted to the signed-in user's own data. (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — account authentication. Only your email, name and profile picture are provided.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads to free users. To serve ads it may collect the advertising identifier and ad interaction data and share them with Google's ad network. In regions that require consent (e.g. the EEA) a consent screen is shown on first launch and can be changed later in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the one-time Lifetime purchase. The developer's server verifies payments with the Google Play Developer API and receives refund and cancellation notifications from Google Play. Google Play keeps the original purchase history; the server stores the purchase records described in 1-1.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymized usage statistics, used only to improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash information, used only to improve stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads configuration values such as update notices. No personal data is sent.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — issues a device token for receiving push notifications. The App stores this token on the server only when you are signed in, and currently sends no notifications with it.
  • Anthropic — generates word auto-fill results and AI study notes. Only the term you type may be passed to the Anthropic API, and only when the server has no stored result; your device ID and account information are not passed on.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google services process data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. International transfers

The Supabase server that stores synced and shared data is located in South Korea (Seoul). Google Sign-In, AdMob and Firebase run on Google's global infrastructure, so your data may be processed on servers outside your country. Terms passed to Anthropic by word auto-fill may be processed on servers in the United States.

8. Security

All communication between the App and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Server data is protected by row-level access control so that only the signed-in owner can access their synced data, and shared word sets are exposed only according to the visibility you chose.

9. Notifications

Study reminders and the autoplay media notification are local notifications scheduled and shown on your device. There are no server push notifications, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

10. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete your data in the App (editing your word list, CSV export (Lifetime), account deletion), stop syncing by signing out, and stop auto-fill and AI study notes lookup requests by turning off word auto-fill in Settings (pronunciation audio for words that already have AI study notes is still downloaded from the server when played, regardless of this setting). For any other access, correction or deletion request, contact us at the email below and we will respond without undue delay.

11. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will notify you through this page and an App update.

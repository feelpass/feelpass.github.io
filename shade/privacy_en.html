Privacy Policy

Shade - Private Display Guard

1. Information We Collect

Shade collects minimal information necessary to operate and improve the app:

  • Filter settings and preferences: Stored only on your device's local storage.
  • Analytics data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, feature usage, and crash reports. This data does not include personally identifiable information.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash logs and device information (device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Advertising ID: Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your preferences.

2. Permissions We Request

Shade requests the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Display over other apps (Overlay): Required to draw privacy filters on top of your screen. This is the core functionality of the app.
  • Accessibility Service: Used to detect the current foreground app for per-app filter settings. This service does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
  • Internet: Required for serving ads, syncing analytics data, fetching remote configuration, and processing in-app purchases.
  • Notifications: Used to provide quick toggle controls for the privacy filter via the notification bar.
  • Foreground Service: Required to keep the privacy filter active while in use.

3. Third-Party Services

Shade uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous usage statistics. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports and device information. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Fetches app configuration from server. No personal data is collected.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes in-app purchases. Purchase information is handled by Google Play. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the privacy filter functionality
  • To display advertisements
  • To analyze app usage and improve user experience
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To process in-app purchases

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local settings are permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to Google's data retention policies.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data:

  • All filter settings are stored in the app's private storage area
  • Communication with third-party services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • No sensitive personal data is collected or stored

7. Children's Privacy

Shade does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

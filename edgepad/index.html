EdgePad (formerly One Swipe) is an Android gesture launcher: swipe a handle on the edge, or tap a floating button, to open a pad of apps, folders, contacts and system actions. Slide onto one and let go to launch it. The controls pad and home screen widgets open there too. Handles, pads and folders are free, with no ads. Runs on Android 10 and later.

Pick an app inside without lifting your finger. Each handle can open its own pad or run an action.

Put a weather or calendar widget in a pad and swipe to check it. Stack several widgets in the same spot.

EdgePad is a good fit if you

  • use a big phone with one hand and want the apps you open most within reach of your thumb
  • want to skip a track, change brightness or turn on the flashlight without leaving the app you're in
  • want a quick look at a home screen widget from inside any app

EdgePad isn't for you if you

  • use an iPhone or iPad. EdgePad runs only on Android 10 and later.
  • don't want to grant accessibility access. Without it, the handles and the pad can't appear.
  • want a launcher that replaces your home screen. EdgePad works on top of the one you have.

FAQ

Why does EdgePad need accessibility access?

It's how EdgePad shows the handles, the floating button and the pad over other apps, and runs system actions like Home, Back, lock screen and screenshot. When you switch apps, it reads the app's name for the Recent apps folder and excluded apps. It reads what's on screen only when you run a Paste action. None of this is sent to the developer or any third party.

Where are my pads and settings stored?

On your device. The developer doesn't receive them. Usage stats record only which kind of pad item you used, never app names or screen content. If Android's own device backup is on, they may be included in your Google device backup, like other app data.

Where does the Google Drive backup go?

To a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only EdgePad can open it, and it doesn't appear among your Drive files. EdgePad can access only this folder, so it can't see or change your other files. Backups go straight from your device to Google and never reach the developer. Drive backup stays off until you connect it.

Is EdgePad free?

Yes. Handles, the floating button, pads and folders, picking without lifting your finger, and slots for apps, contacts, websites and system actions are all free. There are no ads in either version.

What does Premium add?

With a one-time purchase you can add Recent apps folder, Previous app and Current app slots, the controls pad and widget pads. You can also keep the pad from opening in apps you choose or in landscape, lock EdgePad and chosen pad items with a PIN, and back up to a file or Google Drive. The price is shown on Google Play.

Is this the same app as One Swipe (OneSwipe)?

Yes, it's the same app. It's being renamed to EdgePad, so you may still see it as One Swipe on Google Play.

Is EdgePad on iPhone?

No. EdgePad runs only on Android 10 and later. There's no iPhone or iPad version.

Why does EdgePad ask for notification access?

To show what's playing in the controls pad, Android requires notification access. EdgePad reads only the song title, artist, album art and the app that's playing, and doesn't read your notifications. Song details are never saved or sent. Without it, the media buttons still work.

Does it get in the way of the Back gesture?

Where a handle sits, swiping in opens the pad instead of going back. You can change a handle's position and length, or open the pad with a floating button instead.

How do I move to a new phone?

Back up to a file or Google Drive in Settings › Backup & restore (Premium), then restore on the new phone (free). Your app lock PIN and permissions aren't included, so set them up again there. To get Premium back, tap Restore purchase on the Premium screen.

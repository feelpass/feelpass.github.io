Privacy Policy

AppVolume - Per-App Volume Control

1. Information We Collect

AppVolume is designed with privacy as a core principle. AppVolume does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app makes no network requests and contains no analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Configured apps and volume levels: The list of apps you configure and each app's media volume level are stored only on your device's local storage.
  • This information is never transmitted anywhere and is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Accessibility Service

AppVolume uses the Accessibility Service to detect which app is currently in the foreground. The detected app's package name is used only transiently in memory to apply the media volume you set for that app; it is never stored or transmitted. The Accessibility Service is not used to read or collect any screen content.

4. Permissions We Request

  • Accessibility Service: Detects the foreground app and switches the media volume automatically. No data is collected, stored, or transmitted.
  • Query installed apps: Used to display the list of installed apps so you can choose which ones to configure.

5. Third Parties

Because AppVolume collects no personal information, nothing is shared with or disclosed to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

AppVolume does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Privacy Policy

LongShot Marker - Stitched Screenshot Annotator

Summary: LongShot Marker is a free, ad-supported utility for annotating long stitched screenshots. All image processing happens entirely on your device — your photos are never uploaded or sent to any server. The app uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play — LongShot Marker never sees them.

1. Information We Collect

LongShot Marker does not run any custom servers and does not collect personal information directly. The only data collected is what integrated third-party SDKs require to deliver ads and process purchases.

1.1 Images you import

  • Images you select from the photo picker, or share into the app via "Share to LongShot Marker", are read only on your device for stitching, annotation and export.
  • Imported images, edits, and the exported PNG never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded to LongShot Marker servers (we don't operate any) or to any third party.
  • The exported result is saved to your device's Pictures gallery (MediaStore) — only you control it.

1.2 Advertising identifier (Google AdMob)

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) — for frequency capping and personalized/non-personalized ad delivery
  • IP address (used for coarse geolocation by Google's ad systems)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

LongShot Marker contains banner, rewarded, and app-open ads. See Google Ads Policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads from Settings → Google → Ads.

1.3 Payment information (Google Play Billing)

LongShot Marker may offer in-app purchases (e.g. ad removal, premium features).

  • All payments are processed directly by Google Play Store. LongShot Marker never receives or stores payment method information such as credit card numbers.
  • The app only receives the purchase token and SKU (product ID) to confirm purchase status.
  • Receipt verification uses Google Play's standard libraries.

1.4 Local preferences

  • App settings (such as floating-control side preference and current editing session) are stored on-device using Android SharedPreferences and DataStore.
  • This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Children's Privacy

LongShot Marker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If a child uses the app, we recommend configuring AdMob to serve only non-personalized ads.

7. Effect of Denying Permissions / No Network

  • No network: Ads will not load and purchases cannot be made or restored. All core image-stitching and annotation features still work fully offline.
